Storage Near Downtown Hutchinson, KS

Drive-up self storage at 10 E Ave C in South Hutchinson — five minutes from Main Street, the Cosmosphere, and the State Fairgrounds.

Five minutes from Main Street

If you live or work in downtown Hutchinson, our South Hutchinson facility at 10 E Ave C is your closest option. Straight down S Main and across the Arkansas River bridge — door-to-door in about five minutes from the courthouse or the Fox Theatre. Same drive if you’re coming from the Cosmosphere or the State Fairgrounds.

The layout is built for how downtown residents and businesses actually move stuff: wide drive-up lanes, a mix of sizes, and easy in-and-out for moving trucks, U-Hauls, and box trailers.

What downtown Hutchinson folks store here

  • Downtown apartment overflow — the historic buildings on Main are gorgeous but the units are compact. A 5×10 or 10×10 gives you the closet space your loft doesn’t have.
  • Small business inventory — downtown retailers, restaurants and offices staging seasonal displays, filing archives, or event supplies.
  • State Fair season staging — vendors, food-truck operators, and non-profits staging booths and equipment for the September fair.
  • Renovations and downsizing — the older housing stock downtown gets remodeled often. A short-term storage stint keeps furniture out of the dust.
  • Estate transitions — families sorting a parent’s home in the historic neighborhoods around 4th & Main.

Sizes and pricing

The South Hutchinson facility has the widest size range of any of our four locations — 5×10 lockers, mid-size 10×10 and 10×15 units, and roomy 10×20 and 10×30 garage-sized units. Prefer climate-controlled?

If you’re storing furniture, electronics, or documents you’d rather not sit in a hot summer garage, our Hutchinson facility on N Plum Street offers climate-controlled units — about 10 minutes north from downtown.

Common questions from downtown Hutchinson renters

How close is your closest facility to downtown Hutchinson?

Our South Hutchinson facility at 10 E Ave C is about a five-minute drive from Main Street — straight down S Main across the Arkansas River bridge. It’s the closest drive-up facility to the downtown historic district.

Do you have climate-controlled units near downtown?

The nearest climate-controlled option is our Hutchinson facility at 4601 N Plum St — about 10 minutes north of downtown. Every unit there is climate-controlled.

Can I fit a downtown apartment’s worth of stuff in a 5×10?

Yes, comfortably. A 5×10 holds a studio or small one-bedroom loft — bed frame, dresser, sofa, mattress, TV, and a stack of boxes. Bump up to a 10×10 if you have a full one-bedroom or want breathing room.

Do you take moving trucks and trailers?

Yes. The drive-up layout at South Hutchinson is built for it — wide lanes, straight approaches, and space to swing a U-Haul or trailer around.
