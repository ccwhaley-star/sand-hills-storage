Storage in Buhler & Yoder, KS

Drive-up self storage in the Buhler Industrial Park — five minutes from downtown Buhler, ten from Yoder, and a straight shot up K-61 from Hutchinson.

A quiet drive-up facility in the industrial park

Our Buhler facility at 305 Industrial Park Rd sits in the quiet industrial park just north of downtown Buhler. Wide drives, well-lit lanes, and back-your-truck-up-to-the-door access — the layout is built for the way farmers, tradespeople and small-business owners actually move stuff.

If you’re in Buhler, Yoder, Sunflower Estates, or the countryside off K-61 — this is your closest option. Serves Prairie Hills Middle School families, the Yoder shopping crowd, farmers along Buhler Road, and every family within about a 10-mile radius.

What Buhler and Yoder folks actually store here

  • Farm equipment overflow — smaller tools, seed bins, seasonal implements that don’t fit in the shop.
  • Harvest season staging — when the shop fills up in September and October, a 10×20 or 10×30 keeps everything else out of the weather.
  • Small business overflow — contractors, plumbers, and tradespeople staging materials, ladders, and tools between jobs.
  • Household downsizing — families moving from an acreage to town or vice versa.
  • Motorcycles, boats, and small trailers — our 10×30 units fit most.

Sizes and pricing

Every size we offer — 5×10, 10×10, 10×15, 10×20 and 10×30 — is available at the Buhler location. Real-time availability and pricing show the moment you click Rent Today on the Buhler location page. For a quote by phone, call (620) 543-5925.

Common questions from Buhler & Yoder renters

How far is your Buhler facility from Yoder?

About 10 miles, or 12 minutes by way of Yoder Road and K-61. It’s the closest self-storage option for Yoder residents by a wide margin.

Do you have units big enough for a small tractor or ATV?

Yes. A 10×20 comfortably holds most compact tractors, side-by-sides, or ATVs. A 10×30 fits a compact tractor with implements, a boat and trailer, or an SUV plus overflow.

Is the facility gated and secured?

Yes — keypad-controlled gate, well-lit lanes, and 24-hour surveillance. Your unit is locked with your own padlock (or one we sell at the office).

Can I access my unit early in the morning during harvest?

Gate access hours vary by season and location. If you’re running combine crews and need pre-dawn access, call us at (620) 543-5925 and we’ll work with you.
