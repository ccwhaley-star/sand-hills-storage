Buhler Facility FAQs

Where is the Buhler Sand Hills Storage facility?

Our Buhler facility is at 305 Industrial Park Rd, Buhler, KS 67522, with easy access from K-61.

Are units in Buhler drive-up accessible?

Yes — every unit at Buhler is drive-up. No hallways, no elevators — just back up and load.
